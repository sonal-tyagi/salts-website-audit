# SALTs – Website Audit | D2C & E-commerce

## Project Overview

This project presents an independent website audit of **SALTs – Future of Wellness**, a D2C electrolyte brand.

The objective was to analyse the website from an SEO, e-commerce, and user experience perspective, identify areas of improvement, and suggest actionable recommendations to improve product discovery, brand credibility, organic visibility, and the overall shopping experience.

## Areas Audited

**1. Website Structure & Navigation**
- Product discovery and navigation structure.
- Accessibility of product collection pages.

**2. Social Proof & Brand Credibility**
- Integration of social media profiles.
- Opportunities to showcase customer experiences and user-generated content.

**3. Blog Content & E-E-A-T**
- Content originality and search intent alignment.
- Author credibility and content structure.

**4. Product Page Optimisation**
- Trial-size product opportunities.
- Product information and lab report accessibility.

**5. URL Structure & SEO**
- Product URL readability.
- Clean and descriptive URL structure.

## Key Focus

The audit focuses on identifying gaps across SEO, product discovery, content strategy, customer trust, and conversion experience, along with practical recommendations for improvement.
